    The scene replays Wanda bringing Mount Wundagore down on herself at the end of Multiverse of Madness, then keeps going. Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom lifts the rubble off her and says, roughly, “Scarlet Witch, awaken. Your power will be mine.” Wanda gasps and opens her eyes.

    MCU Explained

    The MCU, explained

    The Guide tells you what to watch before Doomsday. This is the reference library for everything else, split into five sections. Open any one and read it like a chapter.

      How sure are we?

      Every fact in MCU Explained carries one of four labels, so you always know how solid it is.

      CanonIt's official

      It happened on screen, or Marvel Studios said it officially. Treat it as fact.

      Example: Thanos snapped away half of all life.
      Soft canonStrongly hinted

      Shown in passing, implied or confirmed outside the films, but never fully spelled out on screen.

      Example: exactly how people cross between universes.
      TheoryA reasoned guess

      What fans, or we, expect based on the clues. Marvel hasn't confirmed it.

      Example: the Young Avengers forming.
      EditorialOur opinion

      Our own rankings and judgment calls, like power tiers and villain threat levels.

      Example: ranking Thanos above Galactus.
